Unmatched ads are recorded in the existing persistent Pilot audit database as `product_trend_queue` entries with `awaiting_product_research`; they are not treated as verified products or sent to publishing/ad stages. Scans are cached for six hours by default, and Meta rate limits/errors or empty responses produce partial/empty trend data without failing the hourly chain.

The trend lane uses `META_AD_LIBRARY_ACCESS_TOKEN` (or the existing `META_ACCESS_TOKEN` fallback), `META_GRAPH_VERSION`, and optional `META_AD_LIBRARY_SEARCH_TERMS`, `META_AD_LIBRARY_MAX_TERMS`, `META_AD_LIBRARY_ADS_PER_TERM`, `META_AD_LIBRARY_MIN_TREND_AGE_DAYS`, and `META_AD_LIBRARY_SCAN_INTERVAL_HOURS`. Existing Apify actors are used for supplier/market sourcing (`APIFY_API_TOKEN` and configured `APIFY_*_ACTOR_ID` variables), not Meta competitor ads; the existing paid-run guard remains closed by default and no Apify actor was started.
